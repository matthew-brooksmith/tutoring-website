How I teach

Most of my sessions operate via Zoom, although in some cases I can meet in person. I focus on building a student's conceptual understanding and utilising basic rules to solve every exam problem.

Typically, I ask which topic the student wants to improve in. Firstly, we make sure the conceptual background is clear. Secondly, we work through a few problems together. I can provide problem sheets and past papers, or the student can bring their own from lessons. If the problems are still difficult, we take link it back to basics: I find making animations and diagrams is a great way to learn.

What I cover

All major exam boards — AQA, Edexcel, OCR and WJEC. I can help with most stages of physics and maths; from GCSEs to the University level.
